The managed model

A business should run on its technology, not around it.

Managed IT means Verrin holds the day-to-day responsibility for your systems. We monitor and maintain them continuously, keep every device to a defined standard, stay on top of security, and are the first and only call when your team needs help. The result is a technology environment that is stable, understood and quietly dependable.

Infrastructure management

Servers, hosting and infrastructure, kept available and current.

The systems your business runs on need constant, careful attention. Verrin administers your infrastructure end to end, monitoring performance, applying maintenance and keeping everything available, so the platform underneath your business is never an afterthought.

  • Server & hypervisor administration (VMware, Hyper-V)
  • Hosting, DNS & domain management
  • Infrastructure monitoring & alerting (RMM)
  • Performance, capacity & availability oversight
  • Patch, firmware & update management
  • Backup & disaster recovery (BDR)
Workplace & device management

Every company device, enrolled, configured and compliant.

Whether your team runs on Apple, Windows or both, Verrin manages the full device lifecycle through modern MDM, from the moment a machine is handed to an employee to the day it is retired. Consistent configuration, enforced policy and clean onboarding, wherever people work.

  • Microsoft Intune & Apple Business Manager
  • Mobile device management (MDM) across Apple & Windows
  • Zero-touch enrolment & configuration
  • Security baselines & disk encryption (BitLocker, FileVault)
  • Application deployment & patching
  • Conditional Access & compliance policies
  • Starter & leaver onboarding / offboarding
