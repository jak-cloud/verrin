About Verrin

A technology partner businesses can rely on.

Founded in Valencia in 2016, Verrin has grown into a managed IT and technology partner for businesses across Europe, trusted to run the systems companies depend on, quietly and well.

Our story

Built on a simple idea: technology should just work.

Verrin was founded in 2016 on the belief that businesses deserve a technology partner, not just a number to call in a crisis. From our base in Valencia, we set out to manage IT the way it should be managed: proactively, carefully and with real accountability.

Since then we've grown steadily, taking on the full technology function for businesses that would rather focus on their own work than worry about their infrastructure, devices or security. Today we support clients across Europe, bringing the same standard of management to every environment we look after, whether it runs on Apple, Windows, or both.

We've stayed deliberately focused. We don't chase every trend or resell every product. We do managed technology properly, and we build long relationships with the businesses we serve.

What we believe

The principles behind how we work.

Accountability over excuses

When we take on your technology, we own it. One partner, responsible for the outcome, every time.

Prevention over firefighting

Good IT is quiet. We invest in monitoring and maintenance so problems rarely reach your team.

Clarity over jargon

We explain technology in plain terms, so you always understand what we're doing and why.

Relationships over transactions

We're in it for the long term. The best IT partnerships are built over years, not projects.

Leadership

Alex De Cruz

Director, Verrin

Alex founded Verrin in 2016 and continues to lead the business today, setting the standard the whole team works to.

His conviction is straightforward: businesses are best served by a technology partner who takes genuine ownership: one that understands the environment in detail, manages it proactively, and is accountable for keeping it secure and dependable. That principle shapes how Verrin works with every client, from a single managed requirement to an entire technology function.

Where we are

Rooted in Valencia, working across Europe.

Our home is Valencia, on Spain's Mediterranean coast. From here we support businesses locally and across Europe, combining on-the-ground presence with technology that lets us manage estates wherever they are.
