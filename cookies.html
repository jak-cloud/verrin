Cookie Policy

What cookies and similar technologies this website uses, why, and how you can manage them.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in the same way. They let a site remember your actions and preferences so it can work properly and understand how it is used.

2. How we use them

This is a lightweight informational website. We keep our use of cookies and similar technologies to a minimum:

Strictly necessary

  • Consent preference. When you accept or decline our cookie notice, we store that choice in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again on every visit. This is required for the site to respect your decision and does not track you.

Third-party resources

  • Google Fonts. We load the Inter typeface from Google's font service, which means your browser makes a request to Google to fetch the font files.
  • unpkg (icons). We load our icon library from the unpkg content-delivery network.

These providers may process technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver the requested files. We do not use advertising, profiling or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not currently run analytics on this site.

3. Managing cookies

You can accept or decline our cookie notice when it appears. You can also control and delete cookies and local storage through your browser settings at any time. Most browsers let you block or remove them from their privacy or security settings. Blocking strictly necessary storage may mean the site cannot remember your preferences.

4. More information

For how we handle personal data more generally, see our Privacy Policy. If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy, email us at hello@verrin.cc.
